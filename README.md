## Key Findings
## 🔍 Key Findings

Based on the Power BI dashboard analysis of 2,895 customer records:

1. **Customer Churn:** The overall churn rate is **24.97%**, with 723 customers churned and 2,172 customers retained.
2. **Internet Service:** Fiber-optic customers have the highest displayed churn rate at **30.42%**, followed by customers without internet service at 26.99% and DSL customers at 16.32%.
3. **Contract Distribution:** Month-to-month contracts represent the largest customer segment, making contract type an important area for further churn analysis.
4. **Monthly Charges:** The average monthly customer charge is **61.88**. Comparing billing patterns across churn segments could help identify potential pricing-related risks.
5. **Retention Opportunities:** Investigate churn among fiber-optic customers, evaluate contract-level churn rates, and assess whether customer support and billing patterns are associated with churn.

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Investigate the reasons behind the higher churn rate among fiber-optic customers.
- Develop targeted retention strategies for at-risk customer segments.
- Evaluate incentives for customers considering longer-term contracts.
- Compare monthly charges and payment behavior between churned and retained customers.
- Monitor churn rate and retention KPIs regularly using Power BI.
